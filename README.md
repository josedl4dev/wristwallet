# WristWallet

Your loyalty cards and tickets, with their QR codes and barcodes, on your watch.

- **Download and install:** https://josedl4dev.github.io/wristwallet/
- **Latest APK:** [wristwallet.apk](https://github.com/josedl4dev/wristwallet/releases/latest/download/wristwallet.apk) (Android 11+)
- **Privacy policy:** https://josedl4dev.github.io/wristwallet/privacy.html

This repository holds the download page and the signed releases of the phone app. The watch app
is installed from the Zepp app's store.
